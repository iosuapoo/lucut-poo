THE FOX MANSION

Trapped inside a sprawling 12-room haunted mansion. Search wardrobes, chests, and desks for the 4 colored keys to unlock the exit door before the Fox monster finds you in the dark.

Survival Guide:

  • Movement: Use WASD or Arrow Keys. Hold SHIFT to Sprint.
  • Flashlight: Press F to toggle flashlight. Keep an eye on battery power!
  • Search: Approach desks, wardrobes, and chests, then press E to inspect them for keys and battery refills.
  • Hiding: Hide inside wardrobes or under beds when the Fox is nearby. The Fox cannot see you inside hiding spots.
  • Escape: Find the Green, Blue, Red, and Yellow keys to open locked doors and the Exit Gate in the Grand Foyer.
