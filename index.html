Game Instructions & Guide

🌾 The Haystack Objective

There are over 15,000 thin straw pieces piled in front of the farmhouse. Somewhere hidden inside is the legendary Golden Needle. Collect hay one by one or click directly on the Golden Needle once revealed!

💰 Hay Rarities & Cash Values

  • Normal Hay (75%): $1 Cash
  • Wet Hay (50%): $2 Cash
  • Grass Hay (25%): $3 Cash
  • Glass Hay: $5 Cash
  • Gold Hay (10%): $7 Cash
  • Rainbow Hay (3%): $10 Cash
  • Diamond Hay (0.5%): $100 Cash

👨‍🌾 The 3 Farm Characters

Farmer Joe (Seller): Stands at the left counter in front of the house to buy your filled sack of hay.
Shopkeeper Bob (Vendor): Stands in the middle shop to sell pitchforks, capacity sacks, cooldowns, and bombs.
The Stranger: Stands near the door waiting for the Golden Needle!
