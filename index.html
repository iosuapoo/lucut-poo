Game Instructions & Guide

🌾 The Needle in a Haystack Search

There are over 15,000 thin straw pieces piled in front of the farmhouse! Somewhere inside is the Golden Needle (1 to 30 min find chance). You can gather hay piece by piece or directly click on the Golden Needle when visible!

💣 Throwing Explosive Bombs

Buy the Bomb in Bob's shop for $1000! Press B or click the Bomb button to throw a physical bomb into the hay field. Upon landing, it creates a visual fireball shockwave that clears hay into your sack (with a 10% chance for triple yield)!
